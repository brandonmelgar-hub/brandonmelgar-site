Laurelwood / The Doñas

Laurelwood / The Doñas is one of Studio City's most distinct pockets, Every street here starts with 'doña' (spanish for 'madam'). Homes here run ~$3.5M.

Every street here starts with 'Doña' (Spanish for 'Madam'). Two features make it rare in the Hills: all utilities run underground for unobstructed valley views, and every street has sidewalks.

What it's like: Upscale mid-century homes built in the 1960s.

What it costs right now: Homes in Laurelwood / The Doñas run ~$3.5M as of 2026, message Brandon for this week's real inventory and comps in this specific pocket.

Who it fits: Buyers who want genuine privacy and estate-level living without leaving the Valley, the kind of buyer who wants space, quiet, and a real hillside address.
