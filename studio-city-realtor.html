A Studio City REALTOR® Who Actually Grew Up Here

I mapped all 22 Studio City pockets myself. And I build houses, so I know what the work really costs.

I am a REALTOR® in Studio City, and I have lived in the Valley my whole life. I grew up in the Woodbridge pocket. Most of the streets I sell on are streets I already knew before I had a license.

There are a lot of agents who cover Studio City. Most of them cover it from somewhere else. Here is what is actually different about working with me.

I mapped Studio City street by street

Studio City is not one market. It is 22 micro neighborhoods, and the gap between two of them can be a million dollars for the same square footage. Colfax Meadows does not price like Studio Village. Longridge Estates does not price like The Grove.

I wrote a guide to all 22 of them. Nobody asked me to. I did it because buyers kept asking me why two houses ten minutes apart were priced so differently, and there was no good answer written down anywhere.

I build houses, not just sell them

I renovate and rebuild homes with my own money. That changes what I see when we walk a property together.

When a buyer asks me what the kitchen costs to redo, I am not guessing from a chart. When a seller asks whether to fix the foundation before listing or credit it instead, I have done both and I know which one actually nets more. When an inspection comes back ugly, I know which items are real and which ones are the inspector covering himself.

Most agents learn that from repetition over twenty years. I learned it by paying for it.

Who I work with

First time buyers who are scared of overpaying. Sellers who want a real number instead of a flattering one. People buying as is or through probate. Landlords and investors. Families relocating into the Valley who do not know one neighborhood from another yet.

I work Studio City, Sherman Oaks, Valley Village, Toluca Lake, North Hollywood, Encino, Van Nuys and Burbank.

What working together looks like

  1. We talk first, no pitch. I want to know your timeline, your number, and what you are actually worried about.
  2. I tell you the truth about your price. Including when it is lower than you hoped. An agent who agrees with every number you say is not helping you.
  3. I show up to the hard parts. Inspections, appraisals, repair negotiations. That is where deals are won and lost, and it is where most agents are on the phone somewhere else.
  4. You get me. Not an assistant, not a team member you have not met. I answer my own phone.
I work as part of Segovia Real Estate Group, a team serving Los Angeles since 2002, under Luxury Collective. So you get me, and you get a bench that has closed hundreds of transactions.
