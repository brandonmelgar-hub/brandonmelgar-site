Who I am

I grew up here.

I grew up in Woodbridge, in Studio City. My dad added a bedroom and a bathroom onto our house, and I still remember the smell of sawdust while they were framing it.

That is the first thing I ever remember loving. Not houses exactly. The building of them. I still walk my yorkie Bruno through Woodbridge Park most weekends, same neighborhood, though he pushes for Beeman Park and he has opinions about which.

My dad has been a mortgage lender my whole life and my sister is one too, so real estate is the only language my family speaks. My dad was always buying property, and I got into this to help him build his portfolio.

So I do two things. I help families cross into the next part of their life, and I buy overlooked houses and give them a second chance. That is where Cup of Equity comes from. Equity is not a word I picked because it sounded good. It is the thing I do.
