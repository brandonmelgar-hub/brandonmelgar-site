Selected work

Some I sold.
Some I built.

Studio City, Sherman Oaks, Valley Village, Encino, Malibu and South Los Angeles. Some I sold for other people. Some I bought, rebuilt and sold myself.

01 · Sherman Oaks

Cumpston street

Bought it. Built it. Sold it.

I bought a 1959 ranch and took it down to the studs. The listing called the olive green room the Gossip Room. The powder room is high gloss oxblood with black glazed tile. Beige is safer. Beige is also forgettable.

02 · Malibu

Pacific Coast highway

Oceanfront · represented the seller

Nearly a thousand square feet of deck built out over the sand.

03 · Valley Village

Saint Clair avenue

Bought it. Built it. Sold it.

Emerald tile, terracotta baths, unlacquered brass, plaster arches. Every one of those was my call. I could have played it safe and painted everything white. Twenty eight days later somebody bought it because it was not.

04 · Valley Village

Milan drive

Represented the seller

A rooftop with turf, string lights and a view of the hills. Not everyone is buying oceanfront. Most people are buying the life they will actually live, and this one has it on the roof.

05 · Encino

Midcentury post and beam

Represented the seller

Real midcentury character, not a spec finish pretending at it. Architecturally the most distinctive house I have sold.

06 · Encino

Collett avenue

Represented the seller

A five bedroom modern farmhouse south of the boulevard, with a proper home theater built into the lower level.

07 · Los Angeles

West 74th street

Bought it. Built it. Sold it.

A 1920s Spanish with its arches, coved ceilings and built in china niches still intact. The easy money says gut it. I kept all of it and painted the dining room terracotta. Original character is worth saving, and buyers pay for it.
