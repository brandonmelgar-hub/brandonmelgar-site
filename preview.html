Is he actually from here?

Fair question. Everyone says born and raised.

Here is what I can tell you instead. I take my dog Bruno, a yorkie, to Beeman Park and Woodbridge Park, which happen to be two of the neighborhoods I write about. None of that goes on a resume. That is sort of the point.
