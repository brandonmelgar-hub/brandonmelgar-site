My Story

I grew up in Woodbridge, in Studio City. My dad bought a classic two bedroom two bath, and then he added another bedroom and another bath. I still remember the smell of sawdust while they were cutting and framing it.

That is the first thing I ever remember loving. Not houses exactly. The building of them.

I still walk my yorkie Bruno through Woodbridge Park most weekends. Same neighborhood. He gets a vote on Beeman Park instead, and he has opinions about which.

Real estate is the only language my family speaks

My dad has been a mortgage broker and a lender my entire life. My sister is a lender too. This is not a career I went out and found. It is what was talked about at the dinner table for as long as I can remember.

My dad was also always buying property. I got into real estate to help him build his portfolio, and that is where I learned the thing that still drives me: a house is a place to live, and it is also a tool.

What I actually do

I help people cross. Renting the first place. Buying the first house. Outgrowing it. Downsizing once the kids are gone. Every one of those is somebody standing at the edge of a big moment, and I get to help them across. I think of myself as a bridge. I am honored to be a small part of people's stories, and that is the part I am actually passionate about. Not transactions. People crossing.

But a roof is only half of it. Handled right, a house is the thing that catapults a family to the next step of their life. That is what growing up around lenders and landlords taught me, and it is the part most people never get told. I want my clients to have somewhere to live and something that works for them.

And I give houses a second chance. When a house has been overlooked, I buy it, take it apart, and put it back together. I have done it for myself, for my family, and for clients. A house the whole street drove past becomes a house somebody wants to live in, and the block is better for it. That is value I am bringing back to my own community, not just a sale.

I love building homes that families will grow in and make memories in. That is the whole thing, and it started with the sawdust.

That is also where Cup of Equity comes from. Equity is not a word I picked because it sounded good. It is the thing I do.

What's My House Worth?

Not what your ZIP code is worth. What your street is worth.

Zillow can tell you what Studio City is doing. It cannot tell you what your block is doing. Send me your address and I will tell you which micro neighborhood you are actually in, what it has done in the last 90 days, and what that means for your number.

Studio City is not one market. It is 22 micro neighborhoods, and the difference between two of them can be millions of dollars. I mapped every one of them myself, street by street. I call them pockets. That is the thing I can do that a valuation tool cannot.

  4. And if you want it, what it would be worth fixed up. I build homes, so I can tell you what is worth doing before you list and what is not. That part is free too.
