Valley Village

Valley Village is one of the quieter corners of the Valley, a small grid of tree lined streets wedged between Studio City, North Hollywood and Valley Glen. It has its own feel, more residential and less trafficked than what surrounds it, and buyers who find it tend to stay.

It is a real market, not a rounding error. Detached homes here have been closing around a median of roughly $1.45M over the trailing twelve months per CRMLS data pulled in August 2026, while listing medians have run closer to $1.14M to $1.19M depending on the month and the mix. That gap is the story: the houses that are actually renovated and well positioned trade far above the asking averages.

What it is like. Mostly single family on modest lots, a lot of 1940s and 1950s stock, plus newer builds and small lot townhome pockets. Colfax Charter is a real draw for families. It is walkable in patches and car dependent in others, and it sits minutes from both the 101 and the 170.

What I have done here. This is a market I have actually transacted in, not one I am describing from a data sheet. I have sold on Saint Clair Avenue and on Milan Drive, one a design forward remodel and one a townhome with a rooftop deck. Two very different buyers, same neighborhood.

Who it fits. Buyers priced out of Studio City who still want a real neighborhood and good schools, and sellers who need someone who knows the difference between a Valley Village street that carries a premium and one that does not.

Common questions

What is the median home price in Valley Village?

Listing medians have run about $1.14M to $1.19M through mid 2026, but verified CRMLS data pulled in August 2026 showed a median close price near $1,445,000 for detached homes over the trailing twelve months. The spread depends heavily on condition and street.

Is Valley Village a good place to buy?

It tends to offer more house and more quiet than Studio City at a lower entry point, while keeping the same freeway access and much of the same everyday amenities. Homes here spent a median of about 48 days on market in July 2026.

What schools serve Valley Village?

Colfax Charter Elementary is the best known and a genuine driver of demand on certain streets. School boundaries matter a lot to pricing here, which is exactly the kind of thing a ZIP code level estimate will miss.
