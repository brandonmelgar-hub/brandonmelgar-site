Toluca Lake

Toluca Lake is a tiny, tightly held enclave of about 3,100 residents wedged between Studio City and Burbank, walkable, quiet, and increasingly the landing spot for buyers priced out of Studio City. Median sale price ran around $1.485M in early 2026, up over 6% year-over-year.

Toluca Lake has become the pocket buyers turn to when Studio City stops making sense on price but they still won't compromise on neighborhood quality. It's genuinely walkable, and its buyer pool skews heavily toward entertainment-industry professionals, writers, producers, directors, and studio executives, thanks to a 10-minute drive to Warner Bros., Disney, NBC/Universal, and Nickelodeon.

What it's like: A small, walkable enclave with a strong sense of privacy despite its size, 3,100 residents total. Tree-lined streets, a real neighborhood center, and consistently high demand relative to how little inventory turns over.

What it costs right now: Median sale price was around $1.485M as of March 2026, up 6.2% year-over-year, with average home values closer to $1.44M. It's currently a strong seller's market, 1.4 months of supply and a 101.4% sale-to-list ratio, meaning homes routinely sell above asking. Multiple-offer situations are common on move-in-ready homes priced $1.2M-$1.8M.

Who it fits: Entertainment-industry professionals wanting a short commute to the major studios, and buyers who got outbid or priced out in Studio City but still want that same walkable, tight-knit feel.

Common questions

What is the median home price in Toluca Lake?

Around $1.485M as of March 2026, up 6.2% year-over-year, with average home values closer to $1.44M.

Is Toluca Lake a buyer's or seller's market?

Seller's market, about 1.4 months of supply and a 101.4% sale-to-list ratio, meaning homes are routinely selling above asking, especially in the $1.2M-$1.8M range.

Who typically buys in Toluca Lake?

A large share of buyers work in entertainment, writers, producers, directors, and studio executives, drawn by the short commute to Warner Bros., Disney, NBC/Universal, and Nickelodeon.

How does Toluca Lake compare to Studio City?

It's become the landing spot for buyers priced out of Studio City who still want walkability and neighborhood quality, generally at a somewhat more attainable price point.
