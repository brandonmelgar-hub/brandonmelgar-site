Woodbridge Park, the Studio City Pocket With the Brady Bunch House

Woodbridge Park is the Studio City pocket where the actual Brady Bunch house sits at 11222 Dilling St. It is also the pocket I grew up in. Homes here run around $1.9 million as of 2026, the streets wind and wave instead of running in a flat grid, and you can still walk to Tujunga Village and Ventura Boulevard.

Most pocket videos I make are about houses I am showing. This one is personal. Woodbridge Park is where I am from, so when I tell you what it is like to live here, it is not a pitch, it is my childhood.

Yes, that Brady Bunch house

The single most photographed house in the San Fernando Valley sits right here at 11222 Dilling St. The exterior used for the Brady Bunch is a Woodbridge Park home, and people still pull up to see it. It is a fun fact, but it also tells you something real about the pocket: this is a postwar, 1930s era neighborhood of character homes, the kind of streets a TV location scout picks precisely because they look like the idea of a Valley neighborhood.

Why the streets feel different here

Most of Studio City is laid out on a flat grid. Woodbridge Park is not. The streets wind and wave, which gives it a quieter, more tucked-away feel than pockets just a few blocks over. You get 1930s single-family homes, newer construction west of Fair Ave, and condos and apartments closer to Vineland. It is one of the more varied pockets in terms of what you can actually buy.

What it costs, and what you get

Homes in Woodbridge Park run around $1.9 million as of 2026. For Studio City, that is real value. You are buying a genuine neighborhood feel, winding residential streets, and walkability to Tujunga Village and Ventura Boulevard, without the top-tier price tag of the hillside or south-of-the-boulevard pockets. It fits a buyer who wants the Studio City address and the community feel, and is willing to trade a flashier street for a more grounded one.
