Farm Boy in Sherman Oaks: The Sushi Secret Hiding Inside a Produce Market

Farm Boy, at 14107 Riverside Dr in Sherman Oaks, is a neighborhood produce market with a sushi bar inside it, and the sushi is the reason people keep coming back. When I stopped in for my video, Farm Boy piled on the fish, the woman at the sushi bar gave us free samples, and the prices were very good. It sits on Riverside Drive near Hazeltine Avenue, along the southern edge of the Fashion Square pocket, and it is exactly the kind of everyday spot that makes a pocket worth living in.

I posted this one with a simple question: local staple or best kept secret? After spending time there, my answer is both. Plenty of people in the neighborhood already know it. Plenty of people who drive past it every day have no idea there is a sushi counter inside a produce market.

What Farm Boy is

Farm Boy is a small market at 14107 Riverside Dr, Sherman Oaks, CA 91423. It sells fresh fruits and vegetables, flowers and prepared food, and the sushi bar is the star. A Daily Sundial food column from Cal State Northridge, published in September 2015, described it as a local grocery known mainly for its sushi: rolls, nigiri, sushi bowls and seaweed salad made fresh throughout the day. That column reported that the store was 16 years old at the time and that the owner was Mark Oh, which would put Farm Boy on Riverside Drive for more than 25 years. The same column also mentioned Korean dishes like chap chae, a sandwich and wrap counter and a juice bar. That was 2015, so check what is on the menu today before you go.

In the comments on my video, people called it a mom and pop shop worth supporting and said the spicy tuna is some of the best in the Valley. I agree with the first part for sure. It is a true mom and pop shop, and places like this are the reason a neighborhood has a personality.

Where it sits: the Fashion Square pocket

Riverside Drive near Hazeltine puts Farm Boy right along the southern edge of the Fashion Square pocket. That pocket runs from Hazeltine on the west to Woodman on the east, and from Riverside on the south to Magnolia on the north. It is named for the Westfield Fashion Square mall, and it has over 600 homes, from original 1930s to 1950s traditional houses to brand new mega builds, on tree lined streets. Homes in Fashion Square run about $1.26M as of 2026.

Just across Hazeltine to the west is Library Square, which I call the most walkable pocket in Sherman Oaks, with homes around $1.4M in 2026. Sherman Oaks Park and the mall are both within walking distance of Farm Boy as well, according to that Sundial column.

Why a Realtor is writing about a sushi counter

When buyers ask me what a pocket is really like, they are rarely asking about square footage. They want to know where they can grab dinner, pick up produce and flowers, and walk to something that feels like theirs. A market like Farm Boy, with fresh food and a counter that has been there for decades, is part of what you are paying for when you buy near it. It is the same reason I wrote about Boneyard Bistro closing on Ventura Blvd: the places on your block shape what your block is worth to the next owner.
