22 Coffee Shops in Studio City

Studio City has 22 verified, currently open coffee shops, spread across Ventura Boulevard, Moorpark Street and Tujunga Village. Here they are, sorted from the aesthetic ones to the ones you actually get work done at, the matcha specialists, and the classics.

This list only includes places confirmed open, with Google and Yelp ratings pulled the same week. A few names have overlapping details worth knowing before you go, more on that below.

For the aesthetic

Alfred Coffee, 12077 Ventura Pl. The But First Coffee flagship, in the Silver Triangle, and the original of the look everyone else on this list is chasing. Opens 6 AM. 4.4 stars on Google across 416 reviews.

Bluestone Lane, 12186 Ventura Blvd. Australian cafe format, flat whites and the AvoSmash. 4.3 stars across 249 reviews.

Civil Coffee, 12833 Ventura Blvd, inside Sportsmen's Lodge. CDMX cafe culture, order the Figueroa. 4.2 stars across 152 reviews.

Coffee and Plants, 12334 Ventura Blvd. House made plant milk pressed daily, and the Dubai Chocolate Latte. 4.3 stars across 167 reviews.

Blank Street, 13273 Ventura Blvd. App first grab and go, and a matcha lemon loaf. One of the newest on this list, still building review volume at 4.3 stars.

For actually getting work done

Saint Coffee Shop, 11706 Moorpark St. The highest rated coffee shop in Studio City on both platforms, 4.7 on Google and 4.7 on Yelp.

Coffee Fix, 12508 Moorpark St. The neighborhood laptop shop, and the highest Yelp review count on this list at 593. Parking is the known complaint. 4.6 stars on Google.

Aroma Coffee & Tea Co, 4360 Tujunga Ave. A Tujunga Village institution with a full breakfast menu and a backyard patio. 4.6 stars across 2,336 Google reviews, the most reviewed shop on this entire list.

Giorla's Coffee Roaster & Wine Bar, 4342 1/2 Tujunga Ave. Roasts in house daily and turns into a wine bar later on. 4.5 stars across 90 reviews.

Peet's Coffee, 12215 Ventura Blvd #101. The only drive through coffee on this stretch. Opens 5:30 AM. 4.7 stars.

Philz Coffee, 13005 Ventura Blvd. Cup at a time custom blends. Opens 5:15 AM, the earliest sit down shop on the boulevard. 4.5 stars.

Laundry Bar, 11400 Ventura Blvd. A laundromat with a real coffee bar, women owned. 4.7 stars.

The matcha specialists

Whata Peach, 11055 Ventura Blvd. The best Yelp score of any cafe in Studio City, 4.8 stars across 628 reviews. Order the matcha einspanner.

Café Matcha, 12070 Ventura Blvd. Alfred's matcha only spinoff. Cloud creams, waffles, and a curry ham pan. 4.4 stars.

rōk coffee and tea, 12265 Ventura Blvd #101. Tea forward, and ranked the #1 matcha spot near Studio City on Yelp.

The classics

The Coffee Bean & Tea Leaf, 12501 Ventura Blvd. The Original Ice Blended. Opens 5:30 AM. 4.5 stars.

Starbucks, 12170 Ventura Blvd. Opens 4:30 AM, the earliest of anyone on this list. Two more Studio City locations at 10901 and 10965 Ventura Blvd.

Yala Coffee, 11824 Ventura Blvd. Middle Eastern sand coffee, brewed in hot sand, plus a fig puree matcha.

Muffin Can Stop Us, 12616 Ventura Blvd. A muffin led breakfast counter where the coffee is the sidecar. 4.5 stars.

Blue Bottle Coffee, 11962 Ventura Blvd. Minimalist third wave, the lowest rated of the big names at 3.8 stars.

Crave Cafe, 11992 Ventura Blvd. Known as the late night one, open to midnight. It's no longer 24/7, so call ahead before counting on it late.

Alfred Coffee (second location), 12930 Ventura Blvd #122. The second Studio City Alfred most locals don't know exists. 4.3 stars.

A few notes before you go

Alfred Coffee actually has two Studio City locations, the original flagship on Ventura Pl and a second one further east on Ventura Blvd that most people never notice.

Civil Coffee's own site lists it at 12833 Ventura Blvd inside Sportsmen's Lodge, even though some directories still show an older Coldwater Canyon address. Go with the Ventura Blvd address.

Giorla's is spelled G-I-O-R-L-A, not Girola, if you're searching for it. It's also one of the few spots on this list that turns into a wine bar later in the day.

Crave Cafe used to run 24 hours and no longer does, so it's worth a call before you head over late.

For the restaurant side of things, see 20 Date Night Restaurants in Studio City.

What is the highest rated coffee shop in Studio City?

Saint Coffee Shop at 11706 Moorpark St, rated 4.7 on both Google and Yelp, the only shop on this list to hit that mark on both platforms.

Where can I get matcha in Studio City?

Café Matcha, Whata Peach and rōk coffee and tea are the three dedicated matcha specialists. Most of the other cafes on this list serve it too, but those three build the menu around it.

What's the earliest opening coffee shop in Studio City?

Starbucks at 12170 Ventura Blvd opens at 4:30 AM, with Philz Coffee close behind at 5:15 AM.

Is there a coffee shop open late in Studio City?

Crave Cafe at 11992 Ventura Blvd was known as the late night option and used to run 24/7. It no longer does, so call ahead before counting on it for a late night stop.
