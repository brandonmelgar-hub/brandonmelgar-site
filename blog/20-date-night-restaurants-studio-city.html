20 Date Night Restaurants in Studio City

Studio City has 20 verified, currently open restaurants that work for a date night, spread across Ventura Boulevard, Moorpark Street and Tujunga Village. Here they are, sorted from candlelit and reservation worthy to casual enough for a first date.

This list only includes places I confirmed were open as of September 2026. A few names that still show up on other lists have closed or are closing, and I've left them out on purpose. More on that below.

For the special occasion

Firefly, 11720 Ventura Blvd. The library room and the candlelit courtyard make this the one people mean when they say the most romantic restaurant in the Valley. New American, $$$, 4.5 stars on Google across 1,761 reviews.

Vignette, 12023 Ventura Blvd. A whole roasted branzino, sourced from the Studio City Farmers Market fifty yards away. California bistro, $$$, and the highest Google score on this entire list at 4.8 stars.

Rosetta Osteria & Crudo Bar, 12345 Ventura Blvd. Crudo alongside the pasta, from the owner of La Loggia's 33 years on the boulevard. Coastal Italian, $$$, 4.5 stars.

ALTO Fire to Table, 12969 Ventura Blvd. Everything cooks over a live hearth right in the dining room. Argentinian, $$$$, the highest price tier on this list.

UOVO Studio City, 12833 Ventura Blvd, inside Sportsmen's Lodge. Hand cut egg pasta. Italian, $$$, and the most reviewed restaurant in this group at 993 Yelp reviews.

The classic dinner date

The Six Social House, 12650 Ventura Blvd. The highest review volume of any restaurant on this list, 4,100 on Yelp. New American, $$. One note: some directories still list it under an old name, The Six Chow House. Call ahead if the listing you're looking at doesn't match.

Black Market Liquor Bar, 11915 Ventura Blvd. Open since 2011 under a vaulted ceiling built from 7,000 bricks, from chef Antonia Lofaso. Gastropub, $$, 4.5 stars across 842 reviews.

Lala's Argentine Grill, 11935 Ventura Blvd. Table bread with chimichurri, and the milanesa napolitana. Argentine, $$, 4.5 stars across 1,414 reviews.

Flavor of India, 12321 Ventura Blvd. The highest rated high volume restaurant in Studio City, 4.8 stars across 3,547 Google reviews. Indian, $$.

Lal Mirch Indian Restaurant, 11138 Ventura Blvd. Open since 2007, spices imported direct. The chicken tikka masala is the one to order. Indian, $$, 4.5 stars on Google and 4.7 on Yelp across 3,600 reviews.

Talesai, 11744 Ventura Blvd. A thin noodle pad Thai that's hard to find anywhere else in the Valley, plus a beef satay. Thai, $$, 4.5 stars.

Mantee Cafe, 10962 Ventura Blvd. A hidden back garden, and mantee dumplings under sumac and yogurt. Armenian and Lebanese, $$, 4.3 stars.

Jon & Vinny's Studio City, 11334 Moorpark St. The Valley outpost of the Los Angeles Italian group, open since September 2024. Note the street: it's on Moorpark, not Ventura. Italian, $$, 4.1 stars.

The casual date night

Burosu Ramen, 12265 Ventura Blvd Unit 102. The spicy Reddo bowl and the brothless Orenji. Ramen, $$, 4.6 stars.

OyBar, 12446 Moorpark St. Jeff's Special, a pastrami filled jalapeño crusted quesadilla. Bar food, $$, 4.6 stars.

Avi Cue, 11288 Ventura Blvd. Started as a pop up and went permanent. Wagyu shawarma on fresh pita. Israeli, $$, 4.3 stars.

Sattdown Jamaican Grill, 11320 Ventura Blvd. On the boulevard since 2009, Black owned, known for the oxtail and jerk chicken. Jamaican, $$, 4.3 stars.

Vitello's Restaurant, 4349 Tujunga Ave. Open since 1964 in Tujunga Village, with a weekend jazz brunch. Italian, $$, 4.4 stars.

Caioti Pizza Cafe, 4346 Tujunga Ave. From Wolfgang Puck's original Spago pizza chef, and the only $ on this entire list. It's also the one with the maternity salad, the one people order to try to induce labor. Pizza, $, 4.4 stars.

The Baked Potato, 3787 Cahuenga Blvd. A jazz club since 1970, live music included, and the entire menu is stuffed baked potatoes. Unlike anything else on this list. $$, 4.7 stars across 1,033 reviews.

A few notes before you book

Reservations matter most at Firefly, Vignette, Rosetta and ALTO, all $$$ and up. The rest generally take walk-ins, though The Six Social House and Flavor of India get busy on weekends given how many reviews they've racked up.

Three names worth knowing are not on this list, on purpose. Boneyard Bistro is in Sherman Oaks and is closing permanently in September 2026. Barrel & Ashes has closed. SunCafe Organic shows as closed on Google but had recent owner activity, so I'm leaving it off until that's confirmed one way or the other. If you see any of the three on someone else's list, that list is out of date.

What is the most romantic restaurant in Studio City?

Firefly, at 11720 Ventura Blvd. The library room and the candlelit courtyard are what people mean when they call it the most romantic restaurant in the Valley.

Is there an affordable date night option in Studio City?

Caioti Pizza Cafe in Tujunga Village is the only $ on this list. Burosu Ramen, Avi Cue, OyBar and Sattdown Jamaican Grill are all $$ and casual enough for a low key night.

Which Studio City date night spots have live music?

The Baked Potato has been a jazz club since 1970 and the whole menu is stuffed baked potatoes. Vitello's Restaurant in Tujunga Village runs a weekend jazz brunch.

Do these restaurants need a reservation?

Book ahead for the $$$ and $$$$ spots: Firefly, Vignette, Rosetta Osteria & Crudo Bar and ALTO Fire to Table. Most of the $$ and $ spots on this list take walk-ins.
