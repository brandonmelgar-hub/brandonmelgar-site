Notes from the Valley

What I'm actually seeing out here, day to day.

I'm out in Studio City, Sherman Oaks, Burbank, Toluca Lake, and Encino almost every day. Showings, inspections, open houses, and the restaurants and streets in between. I film a lot of it. This is where I write it down.

Some of these are about real estate directly: what came up on an inspection, what a pocket is actually selling for this week, what happened on a deal and why. Some are just about the Valley. Both matter. Real estate is local, and local is made of small things.

I also work South Orange County, so every so often one of these is from down there. Comparing the two markets side by side is one of the most useful things I can show a buyer.
