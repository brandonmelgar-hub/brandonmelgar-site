A Netflix Executive's Husband Built a $28 Million Compound Above Fryman Canyon

The newest $28 million listing above Fryman Canyon was built by Doug Prochilo, a writer and producer married to Bela Bajaria, Netflix's chief content officer, and it's his fourth ground up development in that same stretch of hills.

I already covered the most expensive house in Studio City right now, 3405 Fryman Rd at $32.5 million. Viewcrest Rd is the other one. One street over, one canyon deeper into the same pocket, and the story behind why it's for sale is honestly more interesting than the number on the sign.

What's actually there

12309 Viewcrest Rd is seven bedrooms, eleven bathrooms, just under 11,000 square feet, sitting on more than an acre with sweeping views across the canyon. It hit the market this spring at $28,000,000.

Prochilo built it with architect Adrian Koffka and Cynthia Phakos of Koffka/Phakos Design, and landscape architect Ross Woodley of EPT Design. That's a real team, not a spec builder flipping a lot, and it's the kind of pairing you see when a project is built to be lived in, not just sold.

This is the second post I've written this month on an ultra high end Fryman Canyon listing, and that's not a coincidence. Entertainment money building in these hills is basically Studio City's whole identity, going back decades. A Netflix executive's husband building his fourth ground up home two doors from the priciest listing in the neighborhood is that identity playing out in real time.

Why it's for sale

Here's the part that actually humanizes a $28 million listing. Prochilo built this as the family's next home. By the time it was finished, his kids had all moved to New York. So instead of moving in, he's selling it.

Bajaria has been Netflix's chief content officer since January 2024, overseeing the studio behind a huge share of what's actually playing on the platform right now. Between her career and their kids landing on the other coast, the house built for the next chapter simply stopped fitting the life they're living.

What this actually tells a buyer

I write about listings like this because they're a real, current data point on what the top of Studio City looks like, and because Fryman Canyon is one of the pockets I track closely. But I'd be doing you a disservice if I let one $28 million listing stand in for what buying in Fryman Canyon actually looks like.

The vast majority of homes that trade in this pocket are nowhere near this number. Two ultra high end listings sitting at the top of the hill don't move the price of the three and four bedroom homes a half mile down the same canyon. If you're actually looking to buy in Fryman Canyon, that's the real conversation, and it's a far more reachable one than the headline suggests.
