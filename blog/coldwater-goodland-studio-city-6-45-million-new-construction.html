Studio City · The 22 Pockets

Coldwater Goodland

Most homes in Coldwater Goodland run around $2.6 million. On September 18, 2026 a brand new build at 3710 Goodland Ave hit the market at $6,450,000, about $3.85 million more than the typical house on the same street. Same pocket, same single road in. The difference is almost entirely what got built on the lot.

This is one of the 22 pockets of Studio City, and it is the one most people drive right past. It sits just south of Ventura Boulevard off Coldwater Canyon, and if you do not have a reason to turn in, you never will.

One way in, and that is the point

Coldwater Goodland is a long dead end tucked into the hills with no connecting streets. You come in off Coldwater Canyon and there is no other way through. Nobody cuts through here to beat traffic, because there is nowhere to cut through to. The only cars on the street belong to the people who live on it.

That is the whole appeal. It is quiet first and convenient second. You can still get down to Ventura Boulevard from the base of the hill, but you are choosing privacy over a walk to coffee.

Decades old next to brand new

What I wanted to show in the video is the contrast. Walk the street and you pass homes that have been here for decades, then you hit a house finished this year. That is what a pocket in transition looks like.

What the gap actually means

When a builder spends the money to put up a $6.45 million house on a street where most homes trade around $2.6 million, they are betting on the location, not the house that was there before. The quiet, the single road, the hills, and being a few minutes from Ventura Boulevard and Harvard-Westlake. That is what they think people will pay for.

For a buyer, that cuts two ways. If you buy one of the older homes around $2.6 million, you are buying into a street that someone just valued at far more once it is rebuilt. If you are looking at the new build, you are paying for finished, move in ready construction, and it is worth knowing exactly what the existing homes next door trade for before you decide what that is worth to you.

Who it fits

People who want privacy more than walkability. If a street with only one way in sounds like a feature to you, not a limitation, this is one of the few pockets in Studio City that delivers it.

Before you write an offer here

Drive in at 8am and again at 6pm on a weekday. A single road pocket feels completely different at rush hour than it does on a Sunday, and the drive out onto Coldwater Canyon is the part you will do every day. Then ask for recent sales of the older homes on the street, not just the new listing. The new build sets a ceiling. The older homes tell you where the floor is.

How much do homes cost in Coldwater Goodland?

Most homes in the pocket run around $2.6 million as of 2026. That is a pocket level figure, not a specific listing. New construction sits well above it: 3710 Goodland Ave listed at $6,450,000 in September 2026.

Where is Coldwater Goodland in Studio City?

Just south of Ventura Boulevard, off Coldwater Canyon Avenue, climbing into the hills. It is a dead end with no connecting streets, so there is one way in and no through traffic.

Why would a new build cost so much more than the homes around it?

The builder is pricing the location plus brand new construction. The lot, the quiet single road street, and the access to Ventura Boulevard and Harvard-Westlake stay the same. What changes is a 2026 house with a pool, spa and finished landscaping in place of an older home.

Is 3710 Goodland Ave open to the public?

The MLS record showed a public open house scheduled for Sunday, September 27, 2026 from 2 to 5pm. Open house times change, so confirm with the listing agent before you go.
