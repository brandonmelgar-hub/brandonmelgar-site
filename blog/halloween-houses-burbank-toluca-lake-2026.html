13 Halloween Houses Worth the Drive in Burbank and Toluca Lake (2026)

Our nightly walk turned into a Halloween house hunt. This is the list you asked for.

The best Halloween houses near Studio City this year are in Burbank and Toluca Lake. Start at the Toluca Estates Drive cul de sac in Toluca Lake, which closes to cars on Halloween night, then drive about ten minutes to Burbank, where twelve homes within a couple of miles go all out, from a glowing pumpkin cemetery on Ontario Street to a hand built pirate ship on Lima Street. All 13, address by address, are below.

Yen and I walk our neighborhood every night, and this time of year we can't make it a block without stopping, because people get so creative with their houses. One night we grabbed ice cream at Cocoa and Cream, wandered through Toluca Lake, then drove over to Burbank to see a few houses we kept hearing about. We filmed it, asked you to comment SPOOKY for the list, and a lot of you did. So here it is.

Every house on this list was confirmed decorated for 2026 on local home haunt maps or in October 2026 coverage. Most are private homes, so view them from the sidewalk and be kind to the neighbors.

Toluca Lake: the street that closes for Halloween

Toluca Estates Drive and Valley Spring Lane, the cul de sac where the two meet, Toluca Lake. On Halloween night the street closes to car traffic, a security guard is posted, and the streetlights get dimmed so the displays pop. It is a resident run production, not casual yard decor, and it draws costumed families from all over LA. Park on Toluca Lake Ave or Riverside Dr and walk in.

It is a decades old tradition. Bob Hope lived in Toluca Lake and, as the story goes, set the bar early by handing out full size candy bars. Toluca Lake is a fraction of the size of Studio City, but on Halloween night this one block punches way above its weight. More than a dozen other Toluca Lake homes are decorated this year too, including The Bones Home, who found our video in the comments.

Burbank: twelve houses in a couple of miles

The Pumpkin Cemetery, 1135 N Ontario St, Burbank. Glowing jack-o'-lanterns and Halloween lights. Best seen from the sidewalk after sunset, and free. Through Nov 1.

The Buena Vista Haunt, 2038 N Buena Vista St, Burbank. A free walk through maze with handcrafted props, fog, eerie lighting and live actors. Through Nov 1.

Holiday Fantasies Come to Life, 1505 N Valley St, Burbank. A Pirates of the Caribbean theme this year, with full sets, custom props and live animatronics. Built for families. Oct 21 to Nov 1.

Pirates of Burbank, 1226 N Lima St, Burbank. A hand built pirate ship and village, a mermaid grotto, a waterfall and animated Kraken arms. Free. Through Oct 31.

The Nightmare on Orchard, 2352 N Orchard Dr, Burbank. A Nightmare Before Christmas, Beetlejuice and Ghostbusters mashup, with a handcrafted firehouse and jack-o'-lantern arches. Oct 10 to Nov 2.

Hamilton Halloween, 4209 W Jacaranda Ave, Burbank. Towering stacks of glowing jack-o'-lanterns, oversized skeletons, a skull covered arch and fog. Through Oct 31.

Krikorian Light Show, 905 N Rose St, Burbank. Lights synced to music with Halloween themes and pop culture gags. The show spills onto the houses next door. Through Oct 31.

The Burbank Barbie House, 1021 N California St, Burbank. Dolls and miniature sets with a spooky twist, all under pink lighting. A private home, so enjoy it from the street. Through Oct 31.

Bel Aire Manor, 814 N Bel Aire Dr, Burbank. Eerie hand built props and detailed outdoor sets. Past themes include Forbidden Forest and Heaven and Hell. Through Oct 31.

The Holiday Magic Home, 1821 Bonita Ave, Burbank. A free yard display the family rebuilds every season, with past Halloween themes like Nightmare Before Christmas. Through Oct 31.

Circus of Nightmares, 113 S Brighton St, Burbank. An animatronic clown circus with live shows on select nights. Not for the easily scared. Oct 16 to 31, sunset to 10 PM.

Rotten Apple 907, 907 N California St, Burbank. A nonprofit walk through haunted house that has been running for more than 30 years. This year's theme is Twisted Yuletide. Oct 24, 25, 30 and 31 only.

How to do it in one night

Most displays light up around sunset and run until about 10 PM. Halloween falls on a Saturday this year, which is peak night for every house on this list and the only night Toluca Estates Drive closes. If you want to see the Burbank houses without the crowds, go on a weeknight before the 31st, when nearly everything is already up. On Halloween itself, start in Toluca Lake before dark to find parking, then drive to Burbank. The Burbank houses are close enough that you can park once and walk a few of them.

Rotten Apple 907 is only open four nights, Oct 24, 25, 30 and 31, and Circus of Nightmares saves its live shows for select nights, so check their pages before you go.

Why I care about this as a realtor

A street that closes itself down for Halloween tells you something no listing will: the neighbors know each other, they plan together, and they stay. When buyers ask me what a neighborhood is really like, this is the kind of thing I point to. Where is the best trick or treating in the San Fernando Valley?

The Toluca Estates Drive and Valley Spring Lane cul de sac in Toluca Lake is the best known. The street closes to cars on Halloween night, with security and dimmed streetlights. Burbank has the most decorated homes close together, with twelve on this list within a couple of miles.

Are the Burbank Halloween houses free?

Yes. Every house on this list is free to see from the sidewalk, and The Buena Vista Haunt at 2038 N Buena Vista St is a free walk through.

What time do the Burbank Halloween houses light up?

Most turn on around sunset and run until about 10 PM. A few, like Rotten Apple 907, are only open on specific nights, so check before you go.

Does Toluca Estates Drive close on Halloween?

Yes. It closes to car traffic on Halloween night. Park on Toluca Lake Ave or Riverside Dr and walk in.
