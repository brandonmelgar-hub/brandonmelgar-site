22 Breakfast and Brunch Spots in Studio City

Where Yen and I go for Sunday brunch, and every other breakfast spot worth knowing in the neighborhood.

Studio City has 22 verified, currently open breakfast and brunch spots, spread across Ventura Boulevard, Ventura Place, Moorpark Street and Tujunga Village. Here they are, sorted into the quick grab and go mornings, the sit down breakfasts, and the brunch plays, plus the spots outside Studio City from our Sunday breakfast tradition.

Sunday mornings are our thing. Yen and I have a standing tradition: every Sunday we pick a breakfast or brunch spot in the Valley we haven't tried yet and go, just the two of us, a slow morning and whatever the place is known for. One week it's a bagel counter, the next it's a line out the door in Burbank. We started it as a way to explore the Valley together one week at a time, and it turned into our favorite part of the weekend. This list is where we keep track.

This list only includes places confirmed open, with Google and Yelp ratings pulled the same week in September 2026. A few have details worth knowing before you go, more on that below.

The quick mornings: bagels, burritos and sandwiches

Western Bagel, 12930 Ventura Blvd #108. Kettle boiled, about 20 flavors, and it opens at 5 AM, the earliest bagel door in Studio City. 4.2 stars on Google across 232 reviews.

PopUp Bagels, 12184 Ventura Blvd. Grip, rip and dip: hot bagels you tear and dip straight into the schmear. Expect a real 15 minute line on weekends. 4.3 stars on Google.

Muffin Can Stop Us, 12616 Ventura Blvd. Opens 6:30 AM, and the digital waitlist opens with the door. 4.5 stars across 153 reviews.

Victory Caffe, 11040 Ventura Blvd. Go for the breakfast burrito. 4.9 stars on Google across 102 reviews and 4.7 on Yelp.

Coffee Fix, 12508 Moorpark St. Famous as a laptop coffee shop, but the bagel sandwiches are what reviewers single out. 4.6 stars.

Uncle Paulie's Deli, 3990 Vantage Ave. East coast breakfast sandwiches from 8 AM. 4.5 stars across 341 reviews.

Ggiata Delicatessen, 12080 Ventura Pl. An Italian sub shop that also does egg sandwiches, and it has a drive through. 4.3 stars.

Joan's on Third, 12059 Ventura Pl. The marketplace case, the breakfast burrito and the English muffin sandwich. 4.4 on Google, 3.6 on Yelp.

Whata Peach, 11055 Ventura Blvd. Açaí bowls and feta toast, with a 4.8 on Yelp across 628 reviews.

Einstein Bros. Bagels, 12046 Ventura Blvd. The reliable chain pick, known for the Santa Fe bagel sandwich and the avocado bagel. 3.8 stars.

The sit down breakfasts

Art's Delicatessen & Restaurant, 12224 Ventura Blvd. Breakfast all day since 1957. Order the lox plate, hand sliced, with two bagels. 4.1 on Google.

Aroma Coffee & Tea Co, 4360 Tujunga Ave Unit 5. The Tujunga Village breakfast patio, open from 7 AM, and the most reviewed breakfast spot in Studio City at 2,336 Google reviews and 4.6 stars.

Good Neighbor Restaurant, 3701 Cahuenga Blvd W. The mom and pop across from the studios, omelets and the Fast Break. 4.7 stars across 1,190 Google reviews.

Vivian's Cafe, 10968 Ventura Blvd #1/2. An old school Valley diner with a patio. 4.6 stars across 662 reviews.

Sofia's Kitchen, 12747 1/2 Ventura Blvd. Mexican breakfast, and the only perfect 5.0 on Google on this list, across 71 reviews.

Jumpin Java, 11919 Ventura Blvd. All day breakfast with a Mediterranean angle. 4.6 stars.

Great White, 12103 Ventura Pl. Ricotta pancakes and the breakfast burrito, with breakfast served until 3 PM. Book it on Resy.

Highly Likely, 12401 Ventura Blvd. The all day cafe that took over the old Lemonade spot. Chicken and waffles plus serious coffee.

Hugo's Studio City, 12851 Riverside Dr. Health focused all day breakfast with deep vegan and gluten free options. 4.4 stars across 1,346 reviews.

Crave Cafe, 11992 Ventura Blvd. The laptop and late night cafe. 4.1 stars.

The brunch plays

GRANVILLE, 12345 Ventura Blvd A. Steak and eggs. It opens at 11:30, so this is brunch, not breakfast. 4.6 stars across 1,901 reviews.

Brunch & Sip LA Lounge, 11801 Ventura Blvd. Bottomless brunch, the chicken biscuit and lobster mac. 4.7 stars on Google.

Which one should you pick

If you need to be out the door early, Western Bagel opens at 5 AM and Muffin Can Stop Us at 6:30. If you want the best rated plate with real volume behind it, Good Neighbor and Aroma both sit at 4.6 or higher with more than a thousand Google reviews each. For a slow weekend, Great White or Aroma's patio. For a group brunch with drinks, GRANVILLE or Brunch & Sip. And if you just want the best breakfast burrito in the neighborhood, start at Victory Caffe.

Our Sunday brunch picks outside Studio City

Yen and I don't stop at the Studio City line. Some of our favorite Sunday stops so far sit just over it, and these are the ones we'd send you to first.

Café de Olla Restaurant, 2315 W Victory Blvd, Burbank. Our most recent Sunday brunch. A family owned spot with a line out the door, and LA Magazine says breakfast and lunch waits can run past half an hour. Get the chilaquiles and the café de olla it's named after, hot or iced.

Lou the French on the Block, 4007 W Riverside Dr, Burbank, in the Media District. Croissants baked fresh by Lou himself, almond croissants and pain au chocolat. Come early, they sell out.

Marie et Cie, 11704 Riverside Dr, Valley Village. A cozy French inspired cafe with flaky pastries and organic espresso. Time Out lists breakfast and lunch Thursday through Sunday.

Crèma, 13750 Ventura Blvd, Sherman Oaks. Specialty coffee with real latte art, house made pastries and breakfast burritos.

A few notes before you go

Crave Cafe used to run 24 hours. It now opens at 9 AM, so don't count on it for an early or overnight stop.

Google and Yelp disagree a lot on a few of these. Western Bagel is 4.2 on Google but 3.1 on Yelp, Art's is 4.1 against 3.0, and Joan's on Third is 4.4 against 3.6. Worth knowing before you read too much into one score.

Good Neighbor sits in the Universal City Shopping Village, and Hugo's is on Riverside Dr, not Ventura. Both still count as Studio City.

Le Pain Quotidien on Ventura still shows up in search results, but it's permanently closed.

What is the highest rated breakfast spot in Studio City?

Sofia's Kitchen at 12747 1/2 Ventura Blvd is a perfect 5.0 on Google across 71 reviews, and Victory Caffe at 11040 Ventura Blvd is 4.9 across 102. With real review volume behind it, Good Neighbor Restaurant holds 4.7 across 1,190 Google reviews.

What's the earliest breakfast in Studio City?

Western Bagel at 12930 Ventura Blvd opens at 5 AM, the earliest bagel door in the neighborhood, with Muffin Can Stop Us opening at 6:30 AM.

Where can I get brunch with drinks in Studio City?

GRANVILLE at 12345 Ventura Blvd opens at 11:30 for brunch, and Brunch & Sip LA Lounge at 11801 Ventura Blvd does bottomless brunch.

Where is the best breakfast burrito in Studio City?

Victory Caffe at 11040 Ventura Blvd is known for it. Great White and Joan's on Third both serve well known breakfast burritos too.
