Every Valley Breakfast Spot We've Hit So Far

Our Sunday tradition, one breakfast spot a week. This is the running list.

Every Sunday we hit a different breakfast spot in the Valley, one week at a time. So far that's five places across Burbank, Studio City, Valley Village and Sherman Oaks, from a Mexican breakfast spot with a line out the door to a French bakery that sells out of croissants. Here's the full list, and it grows every week.

Every spot on here is one we actually went to and posted about on @cup.of.equity.la. No paid placements, no padding the list.

The sit down breakfasts

Café de Olla Restaurant, 2315 W Victory Blvd, Burbank. Our latest Sunday stop, and the reason this list exists. There was a line out the door when we got there, and LA Magazine says breakfast and lunch waits can run past half an hour. It's worth it. Get the chilaquiles with the café de olla the place is named after, hot or iced. Family owned, and it feels like it.

Highly Likely, 12401 Ventura Blvd, Studio City. The all day cafe took over the old Lemonade spot and opened its fourth location right here in the Valley. The menu runs from breakfast through dinner, with good coffee, brunch and a natural wine list, so this is the pick when half your group wants eggs and the other half wants a drink.

Marie et Cie, 11704 Riverside Dr, Valley Village. A cozy French inspired cafe where we met up with the crew. Flaky, buttery pastries, organic espresso drinks and a real breakfast and lunch menu. Time Out lists breakfast and lunch Thursday through Sunday, with a Belgian waffle on the menu, so this is a weekend move.

The grab and go mornings

Lou the French on the Block, 4007 W Riverside Dr, Burbank, in the Media District. Straight from Paris to Burbank, baked fresh by Lou himself. Almond croissants, pain au chocolat, all small batch. Come early, they sell out. It's a small spot with limited outdoor seating, so most people take it to go.

Crèma, 13750 Ventura Blvd, Sherman Oaks. Specialty coffee with real latte art, house made pastries, and breakfast burritos that make the trip worth it. Cozy and easy to settle into for a slow Sunday.

Which one should you pick

It comes down to how much time you have. If you want a real sit down Sunday breakfast and don't mind waiting, go to Café de Olla and budget for the line. If you're rolling in with a group that can't agree, Highly Likely covers everyone. For a quiet French morning, Marie et Cie on a Thursday through Sunday. If you just need something incredible in your hand before work, get to Lou's early, before the almond croissants are gone. And for a slow coffee with a burrito, Crèma.

The two Burbank spots are a good example of how different one city can feel block to block. Café de Olla on Victory is a family breakfast institution, while Lou's on Riverside sits in the Media District, a few minutes from the studios.

A few notes before you go

Some directory listings put Café de Olla in Los Angeles. It's in Burbank, at 2315 W Victory Blvd.

Marie et Cie's breakfast and lunch menu runs Thursday through Sunday per Time Out, so earlier in the week plan on coffee and pastries.

If you only need coffee, see 22 Coffee Shops in Studio City. That list includes Giorla's Coffee Roaster in Tujunga Village, which we also posted about, and Aroma Coffee and Tea, which has a full breakfast menu of its own.
