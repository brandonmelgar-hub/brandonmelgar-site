Own a Cabin 90 Minutes From LA: The Mountain Home Escape Most Angelenos Overlook

Yes, you can own a real cabin in the San Bernardino Mountains for under $450,000, about a 90 minute drive from Los Angeles. Right now a furnished 2 bedroom cabin in Cedar Glen, minutes from Lake Arrowhead, is listed at $429,900, and it is a good example of what that kind of money actually buys up there.

Most people I talk to in the Valley have never priced out mountain ownership. It sounds like a rich person's hobby, a second home for someone with a first home already paid off. Then you look at an actual listing and the math looks different than the assumption.

The cabin itself

29199 Lake Brook Ave sits in Cedar Glen, a small community a few minutes from Lake Arrowhead. It is a detached cabin, 2 bedrooms and 1 bathroom, 960 square feet, built in 1972, on a lot just over a fifth of an acre. List price is $429,900, and it comes sold fully furnished, so the drive up is closer to move-in day than most home purchases get.

Inside there is a fireplace in the living room, the kind you actually use once the weather turns. Both bedrooms sit on the lower level. Outside, a deck stretches out over the treetops with mountain views, and a circular driveway means parking for more than one car, which matters more on a mountain street than it sounds like it should. Hiking trails start right at the door, there is no HOA, and the property falls in the Rim of the World school district.

What the price history says

This same cabin closed at $280,000 back in 2023 after sitting on the market 61 days, sold to a cash buyer. It is back on the market now at $429,900, furnished. For context, $429,900 is roughly half of what a small condo runs right now in The Grove in Studio City, where units start around $789,900. Leave the city, and the same number buys a freestanding cabin with a deck over the forest instead of a shared wall and an HOA statement.

The honest downside

This is not a house with no tradeoffs. There is no central air conditioning and no garage, so anyone used to Valley comforts should walk in with that expectation set correctly. Mountain access means winter weather can slow the drive up, and the water source and sewer details are still items a buyer should verify directly rather than assume, the way you would with any older mountain property. This is a getaway home first, not a place built for a daily commute.

The listing also carries some short term rental history, which is a real selling point for buyers thinking about income. But the STR permit status with San Bernardino County needs to be confirmed before anyone counts on that income showing up. Buy the cabin for what it verifiably is, not for a number nobody has documented yet.

The point

People assume mountain ownership starts at a price tag that rules them out. It usually does not. Ninety minutes gets you out of the city entirely, and under $450,000 gets you a furnished cabin with a fireplace and a deck over the pines, not a fixer-upper you have to gut first.

If you are curious what a mountain property actually costs compared to what you are paying in the Valley right now, reach out. No pressure, just the real numbers side by side.

How far is Cedar Glen or Lake Arrowhead from LA?

About a 90 minute drive under normal conditions, longer if winter weather is active on the mountain roads.

Does this cabin have HOA dues?

No. There is no HOA on this property, which is one less recurring cost compared to many mountain communities.

Can I rent it out short term?

The property has short term rental history, but confirm the current STR permit status with San Bernardino County before assuming any rental income. Don't buy based on numbers that aren't documented.

Is the furniture included?

Yes, it is sold fully furnished, which is part of what makes it move-in ready rather than a project.
