North Hollywood · NoHo Arts District Northeast

This Classic NoHo House Is Hiding Modern Details Inside

This North Hollywood house looks classic and traditional from the street, but inside every light is either a designer fixture or custom made for the house, the light switches are recessed into the walls, the guest bathroom has a 14 foot ceiling, and a round skylight between the living room, dining room and kitchen doubles as a chandelier. It sits in the Northeast pocket of the NoHo Arts District and it is about 95% done. Here is what makes it different, and why the pocket matters as much as the finishes.

Classic outside, modern inside

The front is what you would expect from an older Valley home: a black front door, brick and iron railings. That was on purpose. The idea was to keep the character the street already has and put the modern thinking inside, where you actually live.

The lighting is the whole story

Every light in this house was chosen for it. The string pendants over the kitchen island are designer fixtures. Everything else, from the dining room chandelier to the hallway and bedroom fixtures, was custom made for this house. Nothing came off a big box store shelf.

Even the light switches are recessed into the walls, so they sit flush instead of sticking out. It is a small detail you feel more than you notice.

A 14 foot ceiling in the guest bath

The guest bathroom ceiling goes up 14 feet, with a skylight at the top. In most houses the guest bath is the smallest, darkest room. This one is the opposite.

The round skylight

The detail I am proudest of is the round skylight. The lights are hidden inside the curve, so during the day it fills the kitchen, living room and dining room with natural light, and at night the whole ceiling glows like a chandelier. We also added skylights through the rest of the house, including in the second bathroom.

Original wood floors

The original hardwood floors were kept and refinished instead of torn out, which is a big part of why the house still feels like it belongs to its era.

Why the Northeast pocket

The NoHo Arts District is not one neighborhood. It is nine pockets, and the pocket changes how the street feels and what you pay. My pick is the Northeast. It is quieter than the blocks right on Lankershim and still close to the Metro and the theaters. You can explore all nine on my North Hollywood pocket map, and read why the Arts District is really nine neighborhoods.
